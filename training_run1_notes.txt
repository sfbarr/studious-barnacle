Observations:
  - Model learned quickly in early epochs; val accuracy improved steadily up to epoch 19.
  - After epoch 19, train accuracy continued climbing toward 100% while val accuracy
    plateaued and slowly declined — textbook overfitting.
  - The train/val gap at the end (~26 points) is large, indicating the model is
    memorizing training examples rather than generalizing.
  - Memory usage stabilized around 5.2-5.3 GB RAM (GPU VRAM usage not tracked here).
  - Epoch time was roughly 1-2 minutes on the RTX 5090.

What to try next:
  1. Weight decay — add weight_decay=1e-4 (or 1e-3) to the Adam optimizer.
     Low effort, often the single biggest regularization win.
  2. Early stopping — best checkpoint was at epoch 19; no need to run 50 epochs.
     Stop if val acc hasn't improved in ~5 epochs.
  3. Learning rate scheduler — e.g. CosineAnnealingLR or ReduceLROnPlateau to
     slow down learning as the model converges instead of blowing past the optimum.
  4. Hyperparameter search — run src/run_hyperparameter_tuning.py to systematically
     test lr in [1e-4, 1e-3, 1e-2], batch sizes in [16, 32, 64], and
     rnn_hidden in [64, 128, 256].
  5. Stronger dropout — currently 0.3 in the FC head only; could try 0.4-0.5,
     or add dropout after CNN layers as well.
